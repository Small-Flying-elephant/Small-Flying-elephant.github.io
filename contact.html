Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What should I do if EverEver rescue emails fail to send?

A: Please ensure you have correctly configured your SMTP email and authorization app password in settings (some providers like 163 or QQ require an authorization code instead of your login password).

Q: How do I verify AdMob app-ads.txt?

A: The app-ads.txt file is provided in the root directory. Simply deploy it to your GitHub Pages root domain so Google's crawler can verify it.
