EverEver (Safety Guardian)

An intelligent safety guardian application designed for families and individuals. Combines one-tap SOS emergency alerts, daily check-in monitoring, GPS location sharing, and automated emergency notifications.

Core Features

One-Tap SOS Emergency

Instantly trigger a countdown SOS alarm during critical emergencies. Automatically calls your primary emergency contact and dispatches distress SMS and emails containing your current GPS location.

Daily Check-In Guardian

Check in daily to confirm your safety. If you fail to check in within the designated timeframe, automated reminders are sent to your family to prevent disconnection risks.

Precise Location Sharing

Automatically obtains high-precision GPS coordinates, attaching clickable map links to emergency emails and text messages so rescuers can locate you instantly.

SMTP Mail & Background Watcher

Supports custom SMTP email authorization. With floating window and background guardian services enabled, SOS emails are dispatched reliably even when the app is closed.
