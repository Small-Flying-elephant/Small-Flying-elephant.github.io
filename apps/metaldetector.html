MetalDetector (Magnetic Sensor)

High-precision metal detection and magnetic field monitoring application utilizing the device's built-in magnetometer. Detect subtle magnetic field changes to locate hidden metals, electrical wires, and magnetic objects.

Core Features

High-Precision Magnetic Sensing

Leverages the device's hardware magnetometer sensor to display real-time magnetic field strength in microteslas (µT) with high precision.

Real-Time Dynamic Waveform

Clear and intuitive real-time magnetic field waveform charts dynamically visualize magnetic fluctuations, making detection scientific and visual.

Audio & Vibration Alerts

When approaching metal or when magnetic strength exceeds threshold limits, the app triggers beep sounds and haptic vibration feedback for precise location pinpointing.

Customizable Sensitivity

Supports manual calibration and sensitivity threshold adjustments to suit different smartphone models and various indoor/outdoor detection environments.
