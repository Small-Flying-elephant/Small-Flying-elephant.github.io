Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Fake Call app (herein referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by lazycat (herein referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service.

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects and stores the following information locally on your device to enable its core functionality:

  • Caller Information: Names, phone numbers, and avatars you provide for simulation purposes.
  • Audio Content: Audio files you select from your device to be played during a simulated call.

Important: All the data mentioned above is stored exclusively in your device's private storage (SharedPreferences and internal folders). The Service Provider does not collect, upload, or share this personal information with any external servers.

Permissions

To provide a realistic call simulation, the Application requires the following permissions:

  • VIBRATE: To allow the device to vibrate during an incoming call.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: To switch between the earpiece and speakerphone.
  • WAKE_LOCK & TURN_SCREEN_ON: To wake up the screen when a scheduled call arrives.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT & SHOW_WHEN_LOCKED: To display the incoming call interface over your lock screen.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Display over other apps): Required on some devices to launch the call interface from the background.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: To ensure the countdown timer remains accurate when the app is in the background.

The Application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID to show personalized ads based on your interests. You can manage these settings in your device's "Google Settings" or "Privacy" menu.

Children's Privacy

The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that his or her child has provided us with information without their consent, he or she should contact us.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Since all simulation data is stored locally, its security depends on the overall security of your mobile device.
