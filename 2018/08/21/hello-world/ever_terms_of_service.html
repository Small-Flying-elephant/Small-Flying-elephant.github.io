1. Description of Services

EverEver is a personal safety assistance tool designed to help users manage personal safety awareness. Its core features include:

  • Daily Safety Check-In: Users confirm their safety by checking in daily. If a user fails to check in beyond a configured timeout period, the App issues local notification alerts.
  • One-Tap Emergency SOS: Triggering an SOS countdown initiates automated emergency email alerts (via user-configured SMTP settings) or phone calls to designated contacts.
  • Emergency Location Helper: Attaching real-time device location map links to emergency emails to assist emergency contacts in locating the user.
