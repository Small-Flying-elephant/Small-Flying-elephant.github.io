Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

LazyCat Studio ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy applies to all Android applications developed by us (including EverEver (Safety Guardian), FakeCall (Simulated Caller), MetalDetector (Magnetic Sensor), and any future applications).

1. Information We Collect

  • Device & Diagnostic Data: When you use our apps, we may automatically collect device model, operating system version, app crash logs, and diagnostic data to help improve app stability and performance.
  • App Core Permissions:
    • Location Permission (EverEver): Accessed only when you trigger an SOS or location sharing feature, used strictly to attach GPS coordinates to emergency emails/SMS.
    • Phone/Contacts Permission: Used for dialing emergency contacts or importing contacts for simulated calls. We do not upload or store your contact data on external servers.
    • Sensor Permission (MetalDetector): Accesses the device's built-in magnetometer for real-time magnetic field measurements processed entirely on-device.
  • Advertising & Identifiers: To support free app operations, we integrate Google AdMob SDK. AdMob may collect Advertising ID and network status information to serve relevant ads.

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve core app functionalities (SOS rescue, fake calls, metal detection).
  • Dispatch rescue text messages or emails to your designated emergency contacts (triggered explicitly by you).
  • Display Google AdMob advertisements and comply with Google Play developer policies.

3. Third-Party Services & AdMob Ads

Our apps incorporate Google AdMob advertisements. Google uses cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on your visits to our apps and other sites. You can manage your ad personalization settings through Google's Ad Settings. To learn how Google uses data when you use partner apps, please visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. Your emergency contacts and SMTP configurations remain stored locally on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that we have inadvertently gathered data from a minor, we will take prompt steps to delete it.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and we encourage you to review it periodically.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact & Support page.
